Method & limits

How we measured it

Scramble throwDropback tagged SCRAMBLE that ended in a pass (complete, incomplete, or INT)
Scramble startFirst moment the QB moves more than 3 yards sideways from where he took the snap
SeparationDistance from the receiver to the nearest defender in coverage
Separation gainedSeparation at the throw minus separation at scramble start
Target / resultReceiver named in the play description; result from plays.csv

Assumptions & limits

  • Tracking ends 0.5s after the throw, so catch-point and yards after catch are not measured; outcomes come from plays.csv.
  • Scramble start is a simple 3-yard rule, not a hand-labeled moment.
  • Separation uses the nearest defender, not necessarily the assigned defender.
  • "Most open" does not account for throw distance or difficulty.
  • The overall effect is statistically significant, but per-player rankings rest on small samples.

Data: NFL Big Data Bowl 2023 — Next Gen Stats player tracking + PFF scouting, 2021 season Weeks 1–8.
